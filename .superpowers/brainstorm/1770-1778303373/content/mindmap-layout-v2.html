Layout Rules

  • Root node centered horizontally and vertically
  • Left children: positioned to the left of parent, evenly distributed vertically
  • Right children: positioned to the right of parent, evenly distributed vertically
  • Connections: horizontal Bezier curves (arcs), not straight lines
  • Grandchildren continue in the same direction as their parent
